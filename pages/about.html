Who We Are

About GB Industrial & Medical Gases

GB Industrial & Medical Gases is an established supplier of comprehensive gas solutions, serving industrial fabricators, manufacturing operations, laboratories, hospitals, clinics, and critical healthcare organizations. We bridge the vital gap between production and application through consistent, reliable gas distribution.

Our core activities encompass the supply, handling, and distribution of industrial gases, medical-grade gases, specialty gases, and essential cylinder equipment. With structured supply schedules and stringent quality assessments, we ensure our clients receive prompt refills and dependable availability to keep essential machinery and clinical systems running without interruption.

Safety and regulatory discipline form the cornerstone of our operations. From high-pressure cylinder testing and segregation to secure loading and compliant transportation, every stage is conducted under strict safety protocols.

Our business philosophy is anchored in long-term relationships, transparency, and personal responsiveness. We treat every client's supply continuity as our direct responsibility, tailoring our delivery agreements to align with operational workflows and emergency needs.

Our Purpose

Why GB Industrial & Medical Gases Was Started

The Unmet Need

In industrial manufacturing and healthcare operations, gas availability is critical. A delay in delivery or a drop in gas quality brings fabrication to an immediate standstill and jeopardizes healthcare procedures. The business was founded after observing frequent supply disruptions, unpredictable delivery timelines, and lack of customer-centered accountability in the market.

Our Core Distinction

From day one, GB Industrial & Medical Gases chose a different path: disciplined adherence to safety standards, rigorous cylinder maintenance, transparent communication, and rapid turnaround for urgent requirements. We set out to be partners who take ownership of the client's continuity.

“[When we started GB Industrial & Medical Gases, the goal was simple: ensure no hospital is left waiting for critical gases and no workshop has to halt production due to supply delays. Reliability and safety are non-negotiable.]”
